What is AI IVR?

Conversational IVR that uses NLU, dialog management and context to understand natural speech and automate resolution without rigid menus.

Core Capabilities

Intent detection, slot-filling, ASR + TTS, sentiment detection, context carryover, and live agent handoff with context.

Business Benefits

Shorter AHT, higher containment, faster routing to skilled agents, personalized responses and 24×7 conversational support.

Who Should Use

Banks, insurers, telcos, utilities, e-commerce and enterprises that need automated, natural-language customer interactions.

Common Use Cases

Balance enquiry, order status, appointment booking, fraud detection alerts, and conversational surveys.

Tech Stack

ASR, NLU models, dialog manager, TTS, vector store for context, webhooks, and integrations with CRMs and knowledge bases.

How AI IVR Works — Flow

  1. Call / Voice Event: System receives call or voice message.
  2. ASR: Speech-to-text converts audio to text in real time.
  3. NLU & Intent: NLU predicts intent, extracts entities (slot-filling) and confidence scores.
  4. Dialog Manager: Decides next action: answer, ask follow-up, escalate to agent, or call API.
  5. Action: Fetch data via API/webhook, read result via TTS, or route with context to agent.
  6. Learning Loop: Interactions are stored for model retraining and continuous improvement.
