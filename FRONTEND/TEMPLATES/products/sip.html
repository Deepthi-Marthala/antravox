Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

Invest small amounts regularly to build wealth over time. SIPs are a disciplined, flexible and convenient way to invest in mutual funds.

  • Rule-based investments
  • Auto-debit
    • See Plans

    What is SIP?

    A method to invest fixed sums in mutual funds at regular intervals — monthly/quarterly — helping rupee-cost averaging and disciplined investing.

    Key Benefits

    Disciplined savings, rupee-cost averaging, power of compounding, low entry amount, and flexible stop/start options.

    Who is it for?

    Beginners, salaried professionals, systematic savers, and long-term investors targeting goals like retirement, education or wealth creation.

    How it Works

    Set amount & frequency → Auto-debit from bank → Units purchased at NAV → Repeats each period → Track in portfolio.

    Types of SIPs

    Regular SIP, Top-up SIP, Flexi SIP (varied amounts), and Trigger-based SIPs (value/threshold or market events).

    Tax & Exit

    Equity funds: long-term capital gains rules apply; debt funds follow different tax rules. Exit loads vary by fund — check scheme documents.

    SIP — Step by Step

    1. Choose Goal: Short, medium or long term (e.g., 3 years, 10 years).
    2. Pick Fund: Select mutual fund & scheme based on risk profile.
    3. Decide Amount & Frequency: Eg ₹500 monthly via ECS/Auto-debit/UPI mandate.
    4. Start SIP: SIP starts from the chosen date; units purchased at NAV.
    5. Monitor & Top-up: Review periodically; use top-up SIP to increase investments.

    SIP Strategies

    Rupee-Cost Averaging

    Buy more units when NAV is low, fewer when high — evens out purchase cost over time.

    Top-Up SIP

    Automatically increase the SIP amount at pre-defined intervals to keep pace with income growth.

    Goal-based SIP

    Allocate SIPs to specific goals with target dates and required corpus computation.

    Flexi SIP

    Vary monthly SIP amounts depending on available funds or triggers.

    Risk & Returns

    Equity SIPs are volatile short-term but historically outperform in long term. Debt SIPs are steadier with lower returns. Diversify across asset classes for balanced risk-return profile.

    Costs & Charges

    Look for fund expense ratio, exit loads, and taxes. Platform charges (if any) are separate — compare total cost before selecting a plan.
