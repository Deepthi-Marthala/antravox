What is Messaging?

A platform to send and receive messages across channels (SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, in-app) for notifications, OTPs and conversations.

Core Features

Multi-channel APIs, templated messages, delivery tracking, fallback routing, inbox for two-way chat and analytics.

Business Benefits

Higher reach, reliable delivery, better engagement, automated flows and measurable campaign performance.

Who Uses It?

Banks (OTP & alerts), e-commerce (order updates), healthcare (appointments), marketing teams (campaigns) and support (conversational chat).

Use Cases

Transactional alerts, OTPs, cart abandonment campaigns, support ticketing via chat, appointment reminders and surveys.

Tech Stack

REST APIs, Webhooks, Provider SMPP/SMPP-like connectors, WhatsApp Business API, Message brokers & analytics pipelines.

How Messaging Works — Flow

  1. API Call: App calls our Message API with channel, template and recipient.
  2. Template & Opt-in: Template checks (WhatsApp) and opt-in verification for compliance.
  3. Channel Routing: Platform selects channel & provider based on rules and fallback policies.
  4. Delivery: Provider delivers message; webhook notifies delivery & failure events.
  5. Conversation: Incoming replies routed to inbox or bot for handling; agents can take over if needed.
